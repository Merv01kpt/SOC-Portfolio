# SOC Metrics and Objectives

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my understanding of Security Operations Center (SOC) performance metrics and how they are used to evaluate the effectiveness of security monitoring, incident response, and operational efficiency.

## Key SOC Metrics

### Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)

MTTD measures how long it takes for security monitoring tools to detect malicious activity after it begins.

A lower MTTD allows analysts to identify threats earlier, reducing the opportunity for attackers to move laterally or achieve their objectives.

---

### Mean Time to Acknowledge (MTTA)

MTTA measures the time between an alert being generated and a security analyst beginning investigation.

Reducing MTTA ensures incidents receive immediate attention and helps prevent unnecessary delays during the investigation process.

---

### Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)

MTTR measures the total time required to contain, remediate and recover from a security incident after it has been detected.

A lower MTTR reduces business disruption, limits attacker dwell time, and improves the organisation's overall security posture.

---

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

Security Operations Centres often operate under predefined Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

These agreements define expected response times based on the severity of an incident. Critical incidents require immediate investigation, while lower-priority events may have longer response windows.

Meeting SLA targets helps ensure that security incidents are handled consistently and efficiently.

## Scenario Analysis

The practical exercises presented operational challenges where different improvements needed to be prioritised.

Examples included:

- Reducing alert response delays.
- Improving analyst workflows.
- Reducing analyst workload.
- Improving notification systems.
- Creating documentation and operational playbooks.
- Identifying the root causes behind poor response times.

Each scenario required selecting the operational improvement that would provide the greatest impact on SOC efficiency.

---

## Lessons Learned

This project reinforced that successful security operations depend on far more than technical detection capabilities.

Even the best detection technology becomes ineffective if:

- Alerts are acknowledged too slowly.
- Analysts become overloaded.
- Processes are poorly documented.
- Escalation procedures are unclear.
- Communication between teams is inefficient.

Continuous measurement of operational metrics enables organisations to identify weaknesses, optimise workflows, and strengthen their overall incident response capability.
